# Heading level one

This is an in-article level-one heading for the showcase. The post title above is the real article heading.

## Heading level two

The interface labels live in Intlayer. Articles and project stories live in MDX, with a separate document for each language. A shared translation key connects related stories even when their URLs differ.

### Heading level three

The visual language uses strong borders, flat color, and hard shadows. For reading, the page settles down: clear headings and a comfortable text column matter more than decoration.

#### Heading level four

You can emphasize a word with _italics_, make a point with **bold text**, or use **_both at once_**. MDX also allows <u>underlined text</u>, <del>deleted text</del>, <mark>highlighted text</mark>, and H<sub>2</sub>O or x<sup>2</sup>.
