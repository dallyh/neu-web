This Privacy Policy explains how I collect and use data when you visit my website, and how I use Umami, a privacy-focused analytics tool.

## Who I Am

I am Dalibor Hon, and I operate this personal website from the Czech Republic. I respect your privacy and am committed to protecting your personal data.
The data centers used to host this website are located in Falkenstein, Germany and operated by [Hetzner](https://www.hetzner.com/unternehmen/rechenzentrum/).

## What Data I Collect

I use Umami Analytics, a self-hosted, privacy-respecting analytics tool. Umami does not collect or store any personally identifiable information (PII).

When you visit my site, Umami collects the following non-personal data:

- Pages visited
- Referring website
- Browser type
- Device type
- Operating system
- Screen resolution
- Country (based on IP, but IP addresses are anonymized and not stored)

You can view the collected data [here](https://analytics.daliborhon.dev/share/PLvxy1iAjOUgirKl/daliborhon.dev).

## How I Use This Data

The data collected is used solely for:

- Understanding how users interact with my website
- Improving content, navigation, and performance
- Monitoring website performance and technical issues

My analytics do not use cookies, fingerprinting, or any invasive tracking technologies.

## Language Preference

When you select a language, I store your choice in the `INTLAYER_LOCALE` cookie for one year. This cookie lets the homepage remember your preferred language on later visits. It is not used for analytics or tracking. You can change the preference using the language switcher or remove it by clearing this site's cookies in your browser.

## Third Parties

I do not share your data with any third parties. My analytics data is stored on my own server, controlled solely by me.

## Data Retention

I retain anonymized analytics data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes above. Since no personal data is collected, no user-specific data is stored or retained.

## Your Rights (for EU/EEA Visitors)

Under the GDPR, you have rights regarding your personal data. However, since I do not collect personal data through analytics, there is no data to provide, correct, or delete.

## Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
