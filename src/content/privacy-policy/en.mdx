## Who Runs This Website

I am Dalibor Hon, based in the Czech Republic. This website is hosted by [Hetzner](https://www.hetzner.com/unternehmen/rechenzentrum/) in Falkenstein, Germany. You can reach me at [contact@daliborhon.dev](mailto:contact@daliborhon.dev).

## Analytics

I use self-hosted Umami to understand which pages people read and improve the website. It records page visits, referring websites, browser and device information, screen resolution, and an approximate country derived from the IP address. Raw IP addresses are not stored in Umami, and the analytics do not use tracking cookies or advertising trackers.

I retain the analytics data to understand readership over time. You can view the [public statistics](https://analytics.daliborhon.dev/share/PLvxy1iAjOUgirKl/daliborhon.dev).

## Contact Form

The form sends your name, email address, message, and selected language through [Resend](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy) to my email inbox. Resend and my email provider process this information to deliver and store the correspondence. I use it to respond to you, and emails may remain archived so I can refer back to earlier conversations.

The form also uses [hCaptcha](https://www.hcaptcha.com/privacy) for spam protection. When the form loads, your browser connects to hCaptcha, which may process your IP address, browser information, and interaction signals. My server checks its verification token before sending the email.

These providers may process information outside the EU/EEA. Their linked privacy information explains their processing and transfer arrangements.

## Comments and Browser Preferences

Comments use [Giscus](https://github.com/giscus/giscus/blob/main/PRIVACY-POLICY.md) and are stored in public GitHub Discussions. Your comments and public GitHub identity are visible to others and remain on GitHub until removed there. The widget connects your browser to Giscus and may load GitHub resources before you expand the comments, even if you do not comment. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for its processing, including international transfers. You can manage your comments through GitHub.

Your selected language is saved in the `INTLAYER_LOCALE` cookie for one year. Your light/dark theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage under `theme` until changed or cleared. These settings remember your preferences and are not used for analytics or advertising. You can remove them by clearing this website's cookies and stored data.
